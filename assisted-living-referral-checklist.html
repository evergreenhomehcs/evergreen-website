For referral professionals

Information that helps Evergreen complete a faster placement fit review.

Complete, current referral information helps reduce delays and allows Evergreen to determine service capability, payer pathway and next steps more efficiently.

Clinical and health information

  • Current diagnoses and recent changes in condition
  • Medication list and medication-administration needs
  • Recent hospital, rehabilitation or clinic records when relevant
  • Ordered monitoring, treatments or therapies

Functional support needs

  • Bathing, grooming, dressing, eating and toileting support
  • Continence needs
  • Walking, wheelchair, transfer and bed-mobility needs
  • Equipment used for safe mobility or transfers

Cognitive, behavioral and safety information

  • Orientation and memory concerns
  • Exit-seeking, wandering, aggression or other behavioral support needs
  • Fall, seizure, aspiration or other relevant safety risks
  • Decision-maker, guardian, POA or authorized representative information when applicable

Payer and timing

  • Private Pay, LTC Insurance or Medicaid waiver pathway
  • Case manager and county information
  • Authorization status when applicable
  • Desired move date and current setting
