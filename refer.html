A local referral option for Anoka County and the Twin Cities

Evergreen is a Minnesota-licensed five-resident assisted-living setting in Coon Rapids. We welcome appropriate professional inquiries from hospital discharge planners, social workers, county and waiver case managers, senior-placement advisors, and family decision-makers. Our direct-care team is on site around the clock with registered nurse oversight; we do not advertise an RN physically on site 24 hours a day.

Referral fit: Services may include daily living assistance, medication management under nursing direction, individualized nutrition support, mobility assistance, safety supervision and care coordination, subject to assessment and licensed capabilities. We consider Private Pay, Long-Term Care Insurance, Elderly Waiver (EW), and CADI/BI when applicable. For CADI/BI customized living at this post-2021 setting, residents must be age 55 or older; final eligibility, authorization and appropriateness are determined individually.

Privacy: Please call first before transmitting resident-level clinical information. Use an approved secure referral channel or the facility's secure fax (763) 762-6132 rather than ordinary marketing email, and share personal information only with appropriate authorization.

What to include

  • Clinical and medication information only through an authorized, secure referral workflow after initial contact
  • ADL, mobility, transfer and supervision needs
  • Cognitive, behavioral and safety considerations
  • Current payer or anticipated payment pathway
  • Desired move date and referral contact information

Payment pathways

Evergreen reviews Private Pay, Long-Term Care Insurance, and Medicaid waiver programs including Elderly Waiver (EW), CADI, and Brain Injury (BI) referrals when eligibility, authorization, service capability and placement fit requirements are met. For CADI and BI customized-living services in Evergreen's post-2021 setting, prospective residents must be age 55 or older.
