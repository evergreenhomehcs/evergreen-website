Professional referrals

Fast, clear placement review.

Case managers, social workers, discharge planners and other referral partners can send referral information to Evergreen for a fit and service-capability review.

Open Referral Form View Referral Packet

What to include

  • Current diagnoses, medications and recent clinical information
  • ADL, mobility, transfer and supervision needs
  • Cognitive, behavioral and safety considerations
  • Current payer or anticipated payment pathway
  • Desired move date and referral contact information

Payment pathways

Evergreen reviews Private Pay, Long-Term Care Insurance, and Medicaid waiver programs including CADI, Brain Injury (BI), and Elderly Waiver (EW) referrals when eligibility, authorization, service capability and placement fit requirements are met.
