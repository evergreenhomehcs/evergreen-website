Website privacy

Privacy information for website visitors.

Evergreen Home Healthcare Services uses this website to provide general information about services, payment pathways, referrals, careers and ways to contact the organization.

Information you choose to send

If you contact Evergreen by phone or email, or follow a link to an external referral or application form, the information you submit is handled through the method or service you choose. Do not send urgent or emergency medical information through ordinary website email links.

Analytics

This website may use analytics technologies to understand general website usage and improve navigation and content. Analytics may collect technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate location derived from network information and pages visited.

External services

Some links lead to third-party services, including online forms, maps and social-media sites. Those services have their own privacy practices.
