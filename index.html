Private-pay pricing transparency

Your rate should reflect the support you actually need.

Evergreen does not present care as a generic package. We first identify the resident’s health-related support, medications, personal care, mobility, behavioral or cognitive support, meals, household assistance, transportation, supervision and staffing needs. Then we develop the proposed private-pay service package and rate.

1

Complete the preadmission assessment

The resident, family member or representative describes current needs, frequency of assistance, routines, risks, equipment and support preferences.

2

Evergreen reviews care intensity

Provider and nursing review identifies which services are needed, how often they are needed, whether one or two staff are required, and where RN oversight or delegated care is involved.

3

Receive an individualized proposal

The proposed rate reflects the assessed service scope and staffing needs. The proposal can show the daily service rate and separate room/rent amount, and the final agreement identifies the services and payment terms that apply.

The small-home difference

A residential setting with room for individual attention.

Evergreen is intentionally small. The goal is not to imitate a large senior-living campus; it is to provide assisted living in a familiar home environment where resident routines, preferences and changing support needs can remain central to daily care.

  • Five-resident capacity in a residential Coon Rapids setting
  • 24/7 staff availability for planned and unplanned needs
  • Resident summoning device available as part of safety planning
  • RN-led clinical coordination for applicable health-related services
  • Meals, snacks, activities and household support incorporated into daily living
  • Family communication and coordination based on resident preferences and authorized contacts

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you choose a home.

How is Evergreen’s private-pay rate determined?

Evergreen first reviews the prospective resident’s current support needs, service frequency, staffing intensity, clinical oversight, personal care, meals, transportation, supervision and other applicable services. The proposed private-pay rate is then developed from that individualized service scope rather than from a generic care label.

Can I complete the preadmission assessment online?

Yes. Use the “Open Private-Pay Assessment” button on this page. The PDF is fillable. You may save the completed copy and return it to Evergreen. Additional records may still be requested before an admission decision and final service plan.

Does completing the assessment guarantee admission or lock in a rate?

No. The preadmission form helps Evergreen understand the resident’s needs. Final services, admission suitability and pricing depend on provider review, required nursing assessment, records, orders when applicable, and the resident’s actual support needs.

Does Evergreen still accept CADI, BI and Elderly Waiver referrals?

Yes. Evergreen continues to review CADI, BI and EW referrals when eligibility, setting requirements, service fit and authorization requirements are satisfied. Private pay is simply the primary focus of this website.

What services may be available?

Depending on assessed needs and provider capability, services may include medication support, health-related assistance, ADLs, mobility and transfers, cognitive or behavioral support, meals and hydration, housekeeping and laundry, activities, transportation coordination, supervision and emergency-response support.

How do I schedule a tour?

Call Evergreen at (320) 469-7560 or email marie@evergreenhomehcs.com. We will discuss the prospective resident’s situation and coordinate a visit.

How can a hospital, county worker, case manager or other professional send a referral?

Professional referral sources may use Evergreen’s secure online referral form or fax documents to (763) 762-6132. The professional referral pathway remains available alongside the private-pay family pathway.
