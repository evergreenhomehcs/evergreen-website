Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you choose a home.

What type of setting is Evergreen?

Evergreen is a licensed 5-bed assisted living home in Coon Rapids, Minnesota with 24/7 staff availability and RN-led coordination for applicable health-related services.

Which payment pathways does Evergreen accept?

Private Pay, Long-Term Care Insurance, and Medicaid waiver programs including CADI, Brain Injury (BI), and Elderly Waiver (EW) pathways are welcomed when applicable. Eligibility, benefits, setting requirements and authorization are reviewed individually.

What services may be available?

Depending on assessed needs and provider capability, services may include medication support, health-related assistance, ADLs, mobility and transfers, cognitive or behavioral support, meals and hydration, housekeeping, laundry, activities, transportation coordination and supervision.

How do I schedule a tour?

How do professionals send referrals?
